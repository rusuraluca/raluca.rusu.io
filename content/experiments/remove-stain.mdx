## Problem

Most stain advice online is generic: search results rarely account for fabric composition, stain age, or how the spill actually happened. People end up guessing and sometimes ruin the garment.

## Approach

Remove stAIn is a multimodal AI web app. Users upload a photo of the stain, specify fabric type (and optional context like “spilled coffee”), and receive a structured cleaning guide: summary, materials needed, and step-by-step instructions.

Gemini’s vision capabilities handle image understanding; the React front-end keeps the flow short and accessible on mobile.

## Outcome

The project shipped as a public demo and **launched on Product Hunt**, validating interest in practical, vision-powered consumer tools built quickly with modern AI APIs.
